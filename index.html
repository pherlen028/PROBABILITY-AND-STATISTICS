The learning journey

Nine units. One foundation.

UNIT 01 · Weeks 1–2

Introduction to Statistics

Nature of statistics; types of data and variables; sampling techniques; tables and graphs.

UNIT 02 · Weeks 3–4

Central Tendency & Dispersion

Mean, median, mode, range, variance, and standard deviation for describing data.

UNIT 03 · Weeks 4–5

Probability

Basic concepts, probability rules, permutations, and combinations.

UNIT 04 · Weeks 5–6

Probability Distributions

Random variables and discrete and continuous probability distributions.

UNIT 05 · Week 8

Normal Distribution

Normal curves, standard normal distribution, z-scores, and the z-table.

UNIT 06 · Week 9

Sampling & Central Limit Theorem

Population, sample mean distribution, and the CLT.

UNIT 07 · Week 10

Estimation & Hypothesis Testing

Confidence intervals, null/alternative hypotheses, p-values, and decision rules.

UNIT 08 · Week 11

Correlation & Regression

Scatter plots, Pearson coefficient, simple linear regression, and predictions.

UNIT 09 · Weeks 12–13

Statistical Software Application

Data entry, management, and analysis using Excel/SPSS.

Culminating Output

Statistical Infographic & Dashboard

An organization wants to educate people about an important issue using statistics. The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful visual information and present it to an audience.
